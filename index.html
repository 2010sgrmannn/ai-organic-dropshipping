Keywords: which ones find real organic / AI dropshipping ads

"Relevant" = the ad is about dropshipping / e-com income and uses an organic or AI angle (SEO agencies, CRMs and other noise removed). Foreplay matches 3-word searches loosely, so the raw count can be high while relevant is 0.

Transcript analysis: how these ads work

Short version: the market is run by AI, not by "organic". About 82% of relevant ads lean on an AI angle (691 of 839); only 345 mention organic, faceless or no-ads traffic. The ads that survive longest pair one big money claim with "AI does the hard part", then take away every excuse ("no face, no stock, no ad spend, 20 minutes a day") and push to a free next step: a masterclass, a free AI store, or a comment-to-DM.

1. The 6 hooks that keep running

  1. Result-first claim (most common in winners). Open on a number before saying who you are.
    "In just three months, my AI store delivered $500,000 in orders. You're next!" (PagePilot, 44 versions, 375 days)
    "Did you know you can earn $5,000 plus a week from TikTok without showing your face?" (Darius Lukas, 54 versions, 271 days)
  2. Rags-to-riches identity. A specific, humble "before" makes the claim believable.
    "Here's how I went from a broke pizza boy to millionaire at 23 with e-commerce without having to spend $1 on ads, without showing my face." (Adrian Dimac, "Organic Dropshipping = Easy Mode", 54 versions)
    "My name is Tommy… a stay-at-home dad who went from getting fired from his 9-to-5…" (Done for you brands, live, 361 days)
  3. Category swap: "it's not X, it's Y". Re-frames dropshipping as the smart investment.
    "The #1 Investment for 2025 ISN'T Crypto or Real Estate… It's AI-Powered Dropshipping!" (Matthew Lepre: still live after 485 days, 20 versions; best performer overall)
    "The best way to make money online in 2025 is not starting an online business. It's having AI start the business for you." (Done for you brands)
  4. Anti-guru / contrarian. Attack courses to sell a free tool or a "real" method.
    "Stop buying courses. You don't need to spend $10,000 on an ecom coach!" (Adrian Morrison, 344 days)
    "Gurus saying they don't do dropshipping, they do AI dropshipping… it's just another get-rich-quick scheme." (The Ecom Business Mentor, 243 days; even the anti-AI angle runs long)
  5. Secret mechanism / "weird trick". The organic angle lives here.
    "The new era of dropshippers are using this weird algorithm trick on TikTok that drives in a boatload of traffic without having to spend a single penny on advertising." (Collins Ecom, 297 days, sold for $10)
  6. POV process demo. Show 3–4 clicks, then "comment W".
    "First go to AliExpress and find the product… Next go to Kopy app… paste… there you have it, a fresh product page." (Kopy.app: 665 days, longest of all)
    "First go to AutoDS and click on trending products… use their AI to generate a free Shopify store… post all videos on TikTok… comment W and I will send you a DM." (autods_tools)

2. The body: the same 4 beats every time

  1. Named proof stack. 2–4 people with job + number: "Ray, a cafe worker… $1 million in sales in five weeks. Nazill, a full-time dad… Jordan… Jess…" (Lepre). Winners name ordinary jobs (cafe worker, student, dad, nursing-school dropout), not "entrepreneurs".
  2. Name a new model. "AI-powered micro-branded dropshipping", "faceless AI dropshipping", "AI e-commerce brand", "AI-driven faceless TikTok system". A fresh label lets them say "the old way is dead" without arguing.
  3. AI does the work. AI finds the product, builds the store, writes the descriptions, makes the ads. "AI is the expert" removes the skill objection.
  4. Kill every objection in one line. "No experience, no design skills, no inventory, no showing your face, no ads, 20 minutes a day, works next to your job or studies."

3. The close: low-friction, free first step

  • Free masterclass / webinar → call or course (Lepre, Tommy/Done for you brands, Dropship Academy NL "for students", Vaka). The most common close in course/coaching ads, which make up ~70% of relevant ad volume.
  • Free AI store (PagePilot, Adrian Morrison, AutoDS, AppScenic). Tools win with "free + limited time" and a dare: "we'll give you $100 if you can set it up faster".
  • Comment-a-word → DM ("Comment W", "comment AI", "DM SCALE"). Runs as organic-looking Reels; very long-lived (Kopy, autods_tools, toozer, Chase Chappell).
  • Tiny front-end offer: Collins Ecom's $10 TikTok strategy, Darius Lukas "80% off". Cheap entry, upsell later.
  • Scarcity everywhere: "free for a limited time", "only 3 days left", "spots are limited", "Shopify may stop paying me for this".

4. What the best performers have in common

  • They scale one concept into many versions: Chase Chappell 55, Darius Lukas 54, Adrian Dimac 54, PagePilot 44, Dropship Academy NL 42+19. Same script, new voiceover or first frame. Duplicating is how they scale a winner.
  • Video, phone-shot, talking head or screen-record: ~74% of relevant ads are video (618 of 839). No polished brand ads in the top 30.
  • One audience callout: "if you're a worker in Australia" (Lepre), "students" (DSA NL), "OFW" (Philippines), "between 15 and 27" (AutoDS). Specific beats broad.
  • Organic used as proof, then fed to paid: "we took this brand from $0 to $500K with little to no ad spend… then put the best TikTok in Facebook ads" (Chase Chappell). Organic is the story; paid is the scale.

5. Organic specifically: the gap

Pure "organic dropshipping" is a thin field: the keyword returns one advertiser (Adrian Dimac, 55 ads, none lasting past 60 days). The organic winners that last frame it as a trick or system (Collins Ecom "TikTok algorithm trick", Darius Lukas "faceless TikTok system") or as a case study (Chase Chappell). The strongest open lane: "faceless AI + organic TikTok". It combines the two hooks that survive (AI does the work, no ad spend), and only a handful of advertisers own it.

6. Keywords worth using

  • Use: "AI dropshipping", "AI e-commerce store / AI Shopify store", "faceless AI e-commerce", "organic TikTok Shop", "free AI store", "dropshipping without ads".
  • Niche but on-target: "faceless dropshipping", "free traffic dropshipping", "viral dropshipping", "dropshipping with AI".
  • Skip as search terms (thin or noisy): "AI organic dropshipping" (returns AI marketing tools, not dropshipping), "organic ecom", "dropshipping no ads".

Method and limits

  • Foreplay API, ordered by longest running, up to 200 ads per keyword: 2,119 unique ads pulled, 839 relevant, 166 distinct concepts from 102 brands, 619 with transcripts.
  • Foreplay has no spend or impression numbers, so "best" is inferred from run time and duplication, not ROAS.
  • Links: Creative = the ad's video/image file hosted by Foreplay (public, all checked 200). Meta = the advertiser's page in the Meta Ad Library (checked in a browser; marked when the advertiser runs no ads now; old inactive ads are no longer viewable one by one on Meta). Foreplay = the ad inside Foreplay (needs an active Foreplay plan).
